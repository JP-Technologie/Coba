About this project

Designed to explore better money tools

Coba is a fictional digital banking interface created to demonstrate how everyday money workflows might look on the web.

What this prototype includes

Sample screens cover a dashboard, transaction history, transfers, bill payments, a Savings Vault, cards, profile settings, and a help assistant. Demo login and verification flows use browser-side interactions.

What it does not do

It does not create financial accounts, store or move money, contact banks or utility providers, issue cards, provide interest, verify identity, or offer regulated financial services.

Why the distinction matters

Sample balances, interest estimates, receipts, and card details are fictional. Front-end checks can be changed or bypassed and must never be treated as protection for real accounts or transactions.

Project status

This is a work in progress. Backend authentication, secure data storage, transaction processing, and operational support are not connected. Do not enter real passwords, PINs, account numbers, or identity documents.
