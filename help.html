Help Centre

Answers about this Coba website prototype.

Is Coba a real bank?

No. Coba is a front-end demonstration, not a licensed financial institution. It does not open real accounts, hold funds, issue cards, or provide banking services.

How does demo login work?

Enter a valid-looking email or phone and any non-empty password. The login page then shows the prototype test code 123456. No email or SMS is sent. Never use a real password.

Did my transfer or bill payment go through?

No real transaction is processed. Transfer and bill forms only create visual demo receipts. Those receipts are not proof of payment, and no bank or provider is contacted.

Does the Vault earn interest?

No. The displayed interest rate and daily estimate are illustrative interface values. No funds are deposited, locked, or paid interest.

Can I freeze or request a real card?

No. Freeze/unfreeze changes only the sample card appearance in the page. Card requests are not issued, ordered, or shipped.

Where are profile settings saved?

Sample profile and notification preferences are stored in this browser only. They are not securely synchronized to an account. Clear site data in your browser to remove them.

Is the demo safe for sensitive information?

No. Do not enter real credentials, payment details, PINs, or identity information. Client-side session checks and attempt limits are demonstrations, not security controls.

Still have a question? Contact the project owner. The form is a local preview and will not send a message.
