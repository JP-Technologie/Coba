Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how this demonstration project handles information displayed by the prototype. Coba is not a real bank and does not provide financial account services.

2. Information in this demo

The login, signup, dashboard, and transfer screens may use browser-held session or local storage for demonstration purposes. Names, balances, account numbers, and transactions shown are fictional or supplied by you for testing.

3. Information collection

The current prototype does not create real user accounts and does not transmit submitted demo passwords or transfer details to an application backend. It may store chat content or session state in browser storage, depending on the feature implemented.

4. Browser storage

Session storage and local storage are controlled by your browser. You can clear site data in browser settings. Closing a session tab may end the session, while local data used by other widgets may remain until cleared.

5. Third-party services

The project may load fonts or scripts from external providers, and hosting platforms may process standard network logs such as IP address and request information. Google and Apple controls shown here are simulations unless a real provider integration has explicitly replaced them.

6. Sensitive information

Do not enter real passwords, PINs, payment card details, bank credentials, identity documents, or financial account numbers. Prototype storage is not designed to protect real financial or identity data.

7. Data control

You may clear browser storage to remove locally stored demo session or chat information. This prototype does not yet provide server-side account deletion or verified data-export processes.

8. Changes

This notice may be updated as prototype features change. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision.

9. Contact

Use the project’s Contact page or the owner’s published contact channel for privacy questions. This notice is a prototype disclosure and not legal advice.
